Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Programming Society — Cyber Cell may handle information when you use this website.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Programming Society — Cyber Cell is primarily an educational website. You can browse most of the content without creating an account.

Depending on the features enabled on the website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide.

  • Name, if you provide it through a contact form.
  • Email address, if you provide it when contacting us.
  • Subject and message content submitted through the contact form.
  • Basic technical information that may be automatically provided by your browser or hosting infrastructure.
We do not ask users to submit passwords, authentication codes, private keys, API keys, or other sensitive credentials through this website.

2. Contact Form Information

If you use the “Send Message to Us” form, the information you enter may be stored so that we can read and respond to your message.

The form may collect your name, email address, subject, and message.

Depending on the final deployment configuration, contact messages may be stored using a database service such as Supabase.

Please never include passwords, API keys, private tokens, authentication codes, financial information, or other confidential credentials in a contact message.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage to remember certain preferences.

For example, the selected website language may be stored locally so that the site can remember whether you selected English, Bangla, or Urdu.

Browser storage is different from a server-side database. Local preferences remain within the user's browser unless the browser or user removes them.

4. Analytics

Analytics services may be added in the future to understand general website usage, such as which pages are visited or which sections are useful to visitors.

If analytics services are enabled, they may process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the website.

However, no website, database, hosting service, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid submitting confidential information through public website forms.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, database storage, fonts, video links, analytics, or other technical functionality.

Examples may include hosting infrastructure, Supabase, Google Fonts, and YouTube.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

7. Younger Users

The platform is intended primarily as an educational resource. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from younger users.

Users should avoid sharing private information, passwords, account credentials, or other confidential information through public forms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services, database configuration, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by the website, you can contact us through the Contact page.
